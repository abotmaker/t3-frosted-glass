# Liquid Glass Third-Party Notices

This T3 Code Android Liquid Glass overlay adapts the Android liquid glass material pipeline
developed for Jarvoice (`abotmaker/Jarvoice`), which incorporates:

- **BitChord / Echo-Music** (`https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord`, rev `1189e6a566c9181111205f26a983f7984f9393ea`, GPL-3.0-only):
  `LiquidGlassRenderer`, `GlassPillMotion`, floating dock & segmented pill lens physics, and knobless `ThinSlider` dimensions.
- **Kyant0/backdrop** (`https://github.com/Kyant0/backdrop` v2.0.0, Copyright 2025 Kyant0, Apache License 2.0):
  Rounded-rect SDF refraction and chromatic dispersion AGSL shader in `GlassShaders.kt`.
- **liquid_glass_easy** (`https://pub.dev/packages/liquid_glass_easy` 4.3.4, Copyright (c) 2025 Ahmed Gamil, MIT License):
  Spring & acceleration-squash constants used in `GlassPillMotion.kt`.
